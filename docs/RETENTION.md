# Evidence retention and privacy

M1 stores accounts, organization membership, imported coordinates, assignments and audit records. It stores no photographs, GPS survey observations or worker background locations. Local development data resides in ignored `.local/`; do not share it as source.

Proposed pilot policy, pending organization approval: original evidence 180 days; derived thumbnails same lifecycle; rejected attempts 90 days unless disputed; review/audit history 2 years; backup expiry 35 days. Legal/contractual requirements must be resolved before evidence collection. Do not implement automatic deletions until the policy owner approves both retention and dispute holds.

M3 private objects need lifecycle rules, revocable short-lived review links and deletion/audit workflows. Offline originals should be removed only after a confirmed server receipt plus validation and a documented safety interval. Browser storage can be evicted or copied by a compromised OS; storage encryption under browser-held keys is not a device compromise defense.

Give workers notice of what location is collected, why, when, access and retention. Collect location during survey capture only. Do not collect continuous routes or background movement. Restrict reviewer access, provide export/rectification workflows and retain immutable original source observations with appended refinements.
