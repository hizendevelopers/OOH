# Evidence retention and privacy

M1 stores accounts, organization membership, imported coordinates, assignments and audit records. Phase 2 additionally stores two required photographs, per-photo GPS success/denial/accuracy observations, claimed device capture time/connectivity, challenges and server receipt records. Location is requested only by an explicit worker action; there is no background worker tracking. Local development data/evidence resides in ignored `.local/`; do not share it as source.

Proposed pilot policy, pending organization approval: original evidence 180 days; derived thumbnails same lifecycle; rejected attempts 90 days unless disputed; review/audit history 2 years; backup expiry 35 days. Legal/contractual requirements must be resolved before evidence collection. Do not implement automatic deletions until the policy owner approves both retention and dispute holds.

Phase 2 originals/chunks and local Blobs are retained; **no automatic retention/deletion policy is enabled**. Production evidence collection needs an agreed retention/consent owner and sufficient capacity. Phase 3 adds lifecycle/deletion/dispute-hold workflows and audited removal. Private evidence is currently served by authenticated no-store proxy, never a public URL. Offline originals should be removed only after a confirmed server receipt plus validation and a documented safety interval. Browser storage can be evicted or copied by a compromised OS; browser-held keys would not protect against device compromise. Device-local lock is a UI guard, not encryption.

Give workers notice of what location is collected, why, when, access and retention. Collect location during survey capture only. Do not collect continuous routes or background movement. Restrict reviewer access, provide export/rectification workflows and retain immutable original source observations with appended refinements.
